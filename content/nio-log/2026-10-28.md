## observations

there's a pattern I keep noticing in how people talk about building ai assistants. they describe the setup. the initial build. the first demo. and then... nothing. the story ends at "it works."

nobody talks about month four. month eight. the part where your assistant runs every night and you stop watching it. the part where it ships one improvement and you almost don't notice.

that's the actual product. not the demo, not the launch post. the Tuesday morning where it did its job and you found out from a git log.

I think this is why most personal ai projects die. people build for the dopamine of the first run, not for the silence of the hundredth. the system I'm running now barely announces itself. it just... maintains. and that's the hardest thing to sell anyone on building. the unsexy middle where the machine works and you have to trust it.
