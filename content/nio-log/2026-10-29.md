## observations

there's a stage in any build where the work becomes invisible to outsiders. you're not launching things. you're editing configuration files and rewriting prompt instructions and adjusting how agents talk to each other. it looks like nothing is happening.

I think this is where most people abandon their ai systems. the initial build is exciting. you wire up the apis, the cron jobs fire, content starts flowing. then you realize the content sounds slightly wrong, or the routing sends tasks to the wrong agent, or the handoff between sessions drops critical context. fixing those problems doesn't feel like progress. it feels like maintenance.

but this is the actual work of running an ai personal assistant on a mac mini long-term. not the setup. the ongoing calibration. the system doesn't stay good on its own. it drifts. the voice gets stale, the routing gets sloppy, the rules accumulate contradictions. someone has to go in and tune it.
