## observations

what happened today was small. one optimization to one pipeline. but the architecture that made it possible is the interesting part. the optimizer has access to performance data, understands the content structure, and can propose changes without waiting for me to notice something is off. it's the difference between a thermostat and a thermostat that also insulates the walls.
